All geology, crossing and target data are stored on the device. USGS Topo tiles are downloaded only when you press the first button. The road download saves Kentucky 911 road centerlines for this map area so “Route here” can calculate a blue road-following line with no signal. Routing is a navigation aid and may include private or restricted roads, so verify access. Satellite imagery and hillshade stay online-only.

Field use

Long-press the map to drop a pin and read its coordinates (4 decimals ≈ 10 m; the 1:24,000 contacts themselves are only good to roughly ±15–40 m). Tap a target for its evidence. Locate uses device GPS.
