Stacker

Merges tripod photos into one: sharp from front to back, with glare and reflections removed, in full resolution. Everything happens on this device — nothing is uploaded, and it works offline once opened.

  • Install it: in Safari tap Share › Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app.
  • Photos: 2–10 shots of the same scene. JPEG, HEIC and camera RAW (DNG, CR2/CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF and more) are accepted.
  • Modes: Focus merges shots focused at different depths. Light keeps your natural light and replaces glare and reflections. Focus + Light does both at once.

How to shoot

  1. Using Stacker: tap Choose photos, select 2–10 shots of the same scene, wait for the result, then tap Save to Photos. Drag the line on the picture to compare with the original.
  2. Tripod + timer. Use a 3 s timer or a remote so nothing moves between shots. Shoot at your camera’s highest quality — Stacker saves at the full resolution of your photos.
  3. Focus stack: focus manually in small steps from the nearest edge to the farthest (Halide, ProCamera, or tap-and-hold AE/AF Lock in the Camera app). Overlap each step with the last by ~30–50%. 5 shots is usually enough; up to 10 for deep stones. Keep exposure locked.
  4. Light stack (glare and reflections): keep the camera and focus fixed and move the light between shots so glare lands somewhere different each time (3–6 shots). Stacker keeps your natural light and replaces only the glare, coloured reflections and shadows. It picks the cleanest photo as the “natural light”; tap ★ on another photo to choose it yourself.
  5. Both at once: change the focus and the light as you go. Stacker takes each spot from a photo that is sharp and clean there.
  6. Glossy agates: glare is best killed optically — a polarizing filter on the lens plus polarized light at 90° (cross-polarization), or a big diffuser. Light stacking helps when the glare moves between shots.
  7. RAW: camera RAW files (DNG, CR2/CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF …) are developed at full quality when your browser allows it. For iPhone ProRAW set Settings › Camera › Formats › ProRAW & Resolution Control → ProRAW Format → JPEG Lossless (Most Compatible); JPEG XL ProRAW can’t be decoded in the browser, so Stacker uses its embedded preview.
  8. Translucent stones: if a focus result looks patchy, try See-through in Adjust, or paint fixes with Touch up.
