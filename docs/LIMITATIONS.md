# Limitations

- **Positional accuracy.** The 1:24,000 GQ contacts meet National Map Accuracy Standards at best (about ±12 m for well-defined points). A contact on a wooded slope is commonly off by 15–40 m, and some are dashed (approximate) in the source; the style is kept in each popup. DEM channels are placed to about one 20 m cell. Four-decimal coordinates (~10 m) are more precise than the geology behind them.
- **Combined-unit quadrangles** (Irvine, Stanton, Slade, Pomeroyton, Heidelberg, Cobhill, Leighton, Frenchburg, Means, Scranton, Zachariah) do not draw the Nada/Renfro contact. The true target sits one Renfro thickness below the mapped line. That thickness was read from the legend for only a few quads (≈5–10 ft in Powell County). Elsewhere it comes from Dever's regional sections (6–17 m).
- **OCR of scanned legends.** Symbols and member names were confirmed for every quadrangle used, but some thickness figures in the OCR text are garbled and were not relied on. Ezel stays AMBIGUOUS because its explanation shows Borden undivided.
- **Wildie belt** crossings are PROBABLE. Where the Wildie is unmapped (Mbh lines), the horizon was excluded rather than guessed.
- **No field verification** was done by this analysis. No clast counts, bar mapping or depositional-trap detection were possible from a 20 m DEM. Hollows that head at the contact are often dry and brushy.
- **Regional input R** uses the KGS agate outline, which partly reflects where people have collected. Its effect is quantified in WHY_THIS_BELT.md.
- **Facies control.** Where silica replacement occurred within the outcrop band is only coarsely known (a handful of measured sections). Scores measure how well a channel samples the horizon, not the local silica abundance.
- **Access and land status** were not evaluated. Most land is private, and parts of Daniel Boone National Forest have their own collecting rules. Check before you go.
- **Offline.** Satellite imagery and hillshade are online-only. Offline topo covers only the areas you download (ranked reaches plus regional zoom 9–12).
